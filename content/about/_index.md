## Why Did I Build This Site?

Over the years, I have read a good chunk of blogs on a variety of topics that interested me. But, after I discovered [Adam Silver's](https://adamsilver.io/) blogs on data-backed UX (User Experience) design theories, I learnt so many valuable new concepts on UX and furthermore, really valued the power of simple text blogs for knowledge-transfer. The idea then crossed my mind to maybe start writing some content of my own for the internet to see, criticise, steal ideas, and so fourth.

This became extra apparent on a project I am currently working on to help optimise my type 1 diabetes management. Specifically, to help call out on other applications that have pretty horrendous UX issues, to the point where I feel can actually negatively affect peoples mental judgement around their care. Once that application hits a workable state and meets my own needs, I will be publishing a blog here to address what it's meant to resolve, and provide example comparisons of its improvements.
